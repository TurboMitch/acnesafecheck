Best Non-Comedogenic Moisturizers for Acne-Prone Skin

A good non-comedogenic moisturizer hydrates without clogging pores. We focus on lightweight, fragrance-free formulas built around acne-safe humectants and ceramides — and avoid the heavy oils and esters that trigger breakouts.

How we choose: picks are screened against our 550+ ingredient comedogenic database for pore-clogging ingredients, favouring fragrance-free formulas with published ingredient lists. What to look for

Acne-safe moisturizer formulas tend to be built around low-comedogenic ingredients such as Hyaluronic Acid, Glycerin, Niacinamide, Squalane, Dimethicone. Use our comedogenic ingredient checker to paste any product's list and confirm before you buy.

Ingredients to avoid

Be cautious with higher-rated pore-cloggers like Isopropyl Myristate, Coconut Oil, Cocoa Butter, Algae Extract. One of these near the top of the ingredient list is a red flag for breakout-prone skin.

FAQ

Does "non-comedogenic" actually mean anything?

It usually means rated 0 on the comedogenic scale, but it isn't a regulated term — a product can be labelled non-comedogenic and still contain a pore-clogger. That's why we check the real ingredient list. Learn more on what comedogenic means.

How do I check a product myself?

Paste the ingredient list into our checker — it flags every pore-clogging ingredient, ranks it 0–5, and marks fungal-acne triggers. Or use the product search to look a product up by name.

A pick broke me out — does that mean the list is wrong?

Not necessarily. Comedogenic ratings are risk signals, not guarantees: concentration, the full formula and your individual skin all matter, and purging from actives can look like a breakout. Patch test new products on one area for 1–2 weeks before full-face use.
