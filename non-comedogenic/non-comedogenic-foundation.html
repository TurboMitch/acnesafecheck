Best Non-Comedogenic Foundations for Acne-Prone Skin

Foundation is a common hidden cause of breakouts because of heavy pigments and esters. We look for oil-free, non-comedogenic formulas that wear well on acne-prone skin.

How we choose: picks are screened against our 550+ ingredient comedogenic database for pore-clogging ingredients, favouring fragrance-free formulas with published ingredient lists. Formulas change, so every pick links to a live ingredient analysis you can run yourself before buying. What to look for

Acne-safe foundation formulas tend to be built around low-comedogenic ingredients such as Dimethicone, Silica, Niacinamide. Use our comedogenic ingredient checker to paste any product's list and confirm before you buy.

Ingredients to avoid

Be cautious with higher-rated pore-cloggers like Isopropyl Myristate, Myristyl Myristate, Octyl Palmitate. One of these near the top of the ingredient list is a red flag for breakout-prone skin.

FAQ

Does "non-comedogenic" actually mean anything?

It usually means rated 0 on the comedogenic scale, but it isn't a regulated term — a product can be labelled non-comedogenic and still contain a pore-clogger. That's why we check the real ingredient list. Learn more on what comedogenic means.

How do I check a product myself?

Paste the ingredient list into our checker — it flags every pore-clogging ingredient, ranks it 0–5, and marks fungal-acne triggers. Or use the product search to look a product up by name.

A pick broke me out — does that mean the list is wrong?

Not necessarily. Comedogenic ratings are risk signals, not guarantees: concentration, the full formula and your individual skin all matter, and purging from actives can look like a breakout. Patch test new products on one area for 1–2 weeks before full-face use.
